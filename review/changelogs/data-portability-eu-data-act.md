# data-portability-eu-data-act: change log

**What happened:** rewritten as part 3 of the SaaS lock-in series. It's now a dated, readable explainer of the Data Act's cloud/SaaS switching rules and what buyers can ask for, with Randy's vendor-strategy view kept. New title: "What the EU Data Act means for SaaS and cloud contracts". Topic: vendors-and-platforms.
- The material repeated from parts 1 and 2 (the Salesforce/Slack/Twitter examples, pricing stats, HubSpot, API-first bullets, MCP stats) was removed.

**Word count:** 3,440 before, about 1,070 after.

## Claims kept or added (with sources)
- The Act applies from 12 Sep 2025; derived data are out of scope for connected-product access; PaaS/SaaS must offer open interfaces and export in a machine-readable format ([Commission explainer](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/factpages/data-act-explained)).
- Art. 23 switching obligations; Art. 25 notice of at most 2 months, a 30-day transition (extendable once), up to 7 months if unfeasible; Art. 29 reduced charges until 12 Jan 2027 then none, including egress, plus pre-contract disclosure; Art. 40 penalties set nationally; Art. 50 dates. All via the eu-data-act.com text mirror (EUR-Lex blocks automated fetch).
- Digital Omnibus: proposed Nov 2025; as of Aug 2026 still in committee, 1,750+ amendments, no Council position ([EP Legislative Train](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/legislative-train/theme-a-new-plan-for-europe-s-sustainable-prosperity-and-competitiveness/file-digital-package)).
- Google Cloud Data Transfer Essentials, Sep 2025 ([Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/networking/new-for-the-uk-and-eu-no-cost-multicloud-data-transfer-essentials)).
- DMA preliminary view on AWS and Azure, Jun 2026 ([DMA](https://digital-markets-act.ec.europa.eu/commission-reaches-preliminary-position-amazons-and-microsofts-market-leading-cloud-services-should-2026-06-25_en)).

## Claims corrected
- "Takes full effect 12 Sep 2025" → phased dates (2025, 2026, 2027).
- "Article 6 right to all derived data" → derived data are out of scope.
- "Article 5 switching" → Chapter VI, Arts 23-31.
- "Continuous real-time portability for business users" → removed (that applies to connected products).
- "2027 penalties up to 4%, GDPR-style" → national penalties; GDPR-level fines only for personal-data matters in other chapters.
- "The Commission will make examples" → enforcement is national.
