# shadow-ai-organizational-intelligence: change log

**What happened:** rewritten, and merged with resistance-to-adoption-ai-change (now a redirect here). New title: "Shadow AI and the approved tools nobody uses". Topic: experimentation. It opens on the paradox that joins the two posts: approved tools go unused while unapproved ones spread.

**Word count:** 2,483 + 2,297 before; about 1,300 after.

## Claims kept or added (with sources)
- 75% of knowledge workers used AI at work, and 78% of those users brought their own tools (Microsoft and LinkedIn, 2024, 31,000 people) ([Work Trend Index 2024](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/ai-at-work-is-here-now-comes-the-hard-part)).
- University of Melbourne and KPMG, 2025, 32,000+ employees in 47 countries: 44% had used AI in ways that go against policy, 48% had uploaded sensitive information to public tools, 61% had hidden their use, 34% said their organization had a generative AI policy ([The Conversation, by the study's authors](https://theconversation.com/major-survey-finds-most-people-use-ai-regularly-at-work-but-almost-half-admit-to-doing-so-inappropriately-255405)).
- IBM 2025: high shadow-AI use added about US$670,000 to the average breach; 63% of 600 organizations had no AI governance policy ([IBM](https://www.ibm.com/think/x-force/2025-cost-of-a-data-breach-navigating-ai)).
- Gallup: most-named barriers were an unclear use case (16%) and legal or privacy worries (15%); 44% of non-users didn't think AI could help; 11% cited reluctance to change; manager support made frequent use 2.1 times as likely, and 28% said they had it ([Gallup](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/694682/manager-support-drives-employee-adoption.aspx)).
- Microsoft 2026 (20,000 AI users): organizational factors explained about twice as much of reported impact as individual effort ([Work Trend Index 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/agents-human-agency-and-the-opportunity-for-every-organization)).
- Self-determination theory (Deci and Ryan), with a 2026 meta-analysis of 192 workplace studies ([Stress and Health](https://doi.org/10.1002/smi.70151)).
- Samsung: restriction reported 2 May 2023 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/05/02/samsung-bans-use-of-generative-ai-tools-like-chatgpt-after-april-internal-data-leak/)); in May 2026 its DX division announced access to ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude from June after a two-month trial with 2,500 staff ([Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/05/26/samsung-opens-doors-to-chatgpt-gemini-plans-phased-humanoid)).

## Claims corrected
- "44% of organizations cannot control unauthorized AI" → 44% of employees, per the Melbourne/KPMG study.
- "75% of workers use AI daily" → 75% of knowledge workers use AI at work (2024).
- "$4.45M shadow-AI breach" → IBM 2025's $670,000 shadow-AI premium.
- Samsung "banned in April 2023; bans were ineffective, staff used VPNs" → restriction in May 2023, and the documented 2026 reversal.
